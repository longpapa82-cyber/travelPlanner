Why myTravel?

Let AI handle the complex planning so you can focus on enjoying your trip

AI-Generated Itineraries

Enter your destination and travel dates — AI analyzes the latest travel trends to create optimized daily plans. Attractions, restaurants, routes, and timing are all carefully arranged. You can also build itineraries manually.

Real-Time Weather & Time Zones

Your itinerary automatically shows local weather forecasts and time zone information for each day. Know when you'll need an umbrella or a jacket, and plan around jet lag before you even leave.

Flexible Editing

Not happy with the AI's suggestion? Freely modify your itinerary — add, remove, or reorder activities. Even during your trip, you can update upcoming plans in real-time for maximum flexibility.

Mobile-First Design

Optimized for smartphones, tablets, and desktops. Especially designed for checking your itinerary on-the-go. Offline caching lets you view your plans even without internet access.

Social & Sharing

Share your travel plans with friends and family, and edit together as collaborators. Browse popular itineraries from other travelers, like and follow for inspiration. Share your own travel stories with the community.

3 Steps to Your Personalized Trip

No endless searching and comparing — just pick a destination and you're done

Choose a Destination

Enter the city or country you want to visit. Quickly select from popular destinations like Tokyo, Paris, or Bangkok, or search for any place. Set your travel dates and group size.

AI Creates Your Plan

AI analyzes recent travel data and local information to build the tailored itinerary. Get detailed daily plans with attractions, restaurants, cafes, and shopping, all organized by time of day.

Customize & Go

Freely adjust the generated itinerary and check weather forecasts. Share your plan with friends or export your data as JSON. Now just enjoy your trip!

🧳 Travel Prep Made Simple

Great trips start with great preparation. Planning your itinerary in advance reduces transit time and lets you experience more. myTravel recommends efficient routes considering travel distances and times. It also provides weather forecasts for your travel dates so you can pack appropriately.

💡 AI-Powered Itinerary Suggestions

myTravel's AI considers your destination, dates, group size, and interests to build a balanced itinerary. It suggests time-of-day appropriate activities — morning walks, lunch at local spots, afternoon attractions, and evening downtime — without overpromising what cannot be verified.
