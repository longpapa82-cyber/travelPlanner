5 Tips for Planning Your Trip with AI

2026-02-15 | myTravel Team

Planning a trip is exciting, but it can also take a lot of time and effort. There is so much to think about: where to go, what order to visit places in, how to get around locally, what the weather will be like, and more. In the past, you had to read through dozens of travel blogs and spread out a map to work out your route piece by piece. Now, thanks to advances in AI, you can handle all of this far more efficiently.

An AI travel planner analyzes vast amounts of travel data and builds a well-tuned itinerary for you. But to get real value out of AI, you need a few pointers. The quality of the result depends heavily on how you feed information to the AI. In this article, we will walk you through 5 core tips to make the most of an AI travel planner in detail. When you use AI-powered travel tools like myTravel, applying these tips will help you get a tailored itinerary that feels like it was put together by a professional travel planner.

Tip 1: Enter Specific Trip Details

The quality of an AI travel planner's output is directly proportional to how specific your input is. Entering just "3-night, 4-day trip to Tokyo" produces very different results than entering "3 nights, 4 days in Tokyo, a couple, budget around $1,000, focused on food and shopping, centered on Harajuku and Shibuya."

Here are the key pieces of information to enter specifically. First is the exact travel dates. Instead of simply "March," entering specific dates like "March 15 to March 18" lets the AI build an itinerary that reflects local events, festivals, and public holidays for that period. For example, if you visit Tokyo in late March, you might get recommendations for a stroll through Ueno Park or along the Meguro River in time for cherry blossom season.

Next, the number and makeup of travelers matters too. Whether you are traveling solo, as a couple, or as a family completely changes the recommended places and restaurants. For a family trip with children, you can prioritize kid-friendly spots, and for a couple's trip, you can get recommendations for romantic restaurants and night-view spots.

Your travel budget is also key input. When you specify a budget, the AI suggests the best options within that range. It adjusts the itinerary to fit your budget by recommending, say, a good-value eatery locals love instead of a high-end omakase, or a clean business hotel instead of a luxury one.

Finally, tell the AI your interests and preferences. The more specifically you enter things like whether you love art museums, prefer natural scenery, are focused on tracking down great food, or care about shopping, the more satisfying an itinerary you will receive. In myTravel, you can select these interests when creating a trip, so the AI builds an itinerary that fits your taste closely.

Practical tip: When you create a new trip in myTravel, selecting your travel style (sightseeing, relaxation, food, shopping, and so on) in addition to the destination and dates helps the AI generate a more accurate itinerary.

Tip 2: Customize Based on the AI's Suggested Itinerary

The itinerary the AI generates is a great starting point, but it is not the final plan. The key is to treat the AI's suggestion as a "draft" and customize it to fit your own travel style. The AI recommends the most popular and efficient itinerary based on data, but it is hard for it to fully reflect your personal stamina, travel pace, and special interests.

First, take a look at the overall flow of the itinerary the AI recommended. Check where the day starts in the morning and where it wraps up in the evening, and how many places you visit each day. If you are not a morning person, you can push back the start time of your first activity and add evening activities instead. Conversely, if you tend to wake up early, try adding a morning walk or an early-morning market visit.

Also, do not be afraid to boldly drop the places the AI recommended that interest you less and add the spots you have always wanted to visit instead. In myTravel, you can freely edit, delete, and add each item in the itinerary, making customization very simple. It is especially important to secure some "free time." Because the AI tries to build an efficient itinerary, the schedule can feel tight, but leaving time here and there to rest at a cafe or explore an alley you stumble upon makes the trip far more satisfying.

Try adjusting the intensity of each day, too. If the AI recommended 5 to 6 places a day, cutting it down to 3 or 4 and spending more time at each place is a good approach. Museums and art galleries in particular often keep you longer than expected, so it is wise to allow generous time. A trip should be a leisurely walk, not a marathon.

Tip 3: Make Use of Local Weather and Time-Zone Information

One big advantage of an AI travel planner is that it provides local weather and time-zone information in real time. Actively using this information makes a big difference in the quality of your trip. myTravel automatically displays the local weather forecast and time-zone information for each date in your itinerary, so you can check it at a glance without searching separately.

Adjusting your itinerary using weather information is very practical. For example, on a day when rain is forecast, you can place indoor attractions (museums, shopping centers, indoor theme parks, and so on) instead of outdoor activities, and on clear days you can concentrate outdoor activities like beaches, parks, and observation decks. This strategy is especially effective in regions with distinct wet and dry seasons, such as Southeast Asia. If it is a period when afternoon squalls roll in, you can adjust by concentrating outdoor activities in the morning and placing indoor plans in the afternoon.

Managing time-zone changes is another important travel tip. On long-haul trips, failing to adjust to the time difference can waste your first few days. After checking the AI planner's time-zone information, placing a light schedule on your arrival day and adjusting your meal and sleep times to local time makes adapting far easier. For example, on a trip to Europe, putting in just a light walk and cafe visit in the afternoon of your arrival day and going to bed around 9 or 10 p.m. local time will have you feeling better from the next day on.

Practical tip: Check myTravel's weather widget and jot down a "Plan B" itinerary in advance for rainy days. A flexible plan is the best plan.

Tip 4: Build an Efficient Route That Accounts for Travel Time

The most wasteful use of time on a trip is transit time. No matter how many great attractions you visit, if travel time is excessive, you end up short on time to actually enjoy the place. An AI travel planner calculates the travel time and distance between each spot to suggest an efficient route, but there are ways to optimize it even further.

First, plan by grouping your itinerary by area. Visiting attractions that are in the same area together on one day can greatly reduce travel time. For a trip to Tokyo, for example, that means grouping Asakusa and Ueno on the same day, and Shibuya and Harajuku on the same day. The AI often recommends things this way already, but you can check for yourself and adjust it to be even more efficient.

Second, compare travel times by mode of transport. In big cities the subway is fast, but when attractions are close to each other, walking can be faster and let you take in some nice scenery along the way. myTravel displays the travel time between each spot, so use it as a reference to choose the best option among walking, public transit, and taxi. Walking is especially the best way to enjoy the old towns of Europe or the alley culture of Japan.

Third, avoid rush hour. Public transit during commuting hours in big cities is very crowded. Traveling outside these hours, or scheduling a meal near your accommodation or a rest at a cafe during rush hour, makes for a much more comfortable trip. Typically the most crowded times are 7:30 to 9 a.m. and 5:30 to 7 p.m.

Fourth, choose your accommodation location strategically. The closer your accommodation is to the main attractions, the more you can cut down on the time spent traveling each morning. Especially for an itinerary that visits several areas, booking accommodation near a transit hub (a central station, major transfer station, and so on) lets you move quickly to anywhere.

Tip 5: Reference Other Travelers' Itineraries with Social Features

Do not try to plan everything by yourself. Other travelers' experiences are among the most valuable travel information out there. Using myTravel's social features, you can reference the actual itineraries of travelers who have been to the same destination. This becomes one of the most reliable sources of information, combining the AI's data-driven recommendations with the vivid, real-world experience of actual travelers.

Through the explore popular itineraries feature, check out the itineraries that received the most "likes" for a specific destination. There is a reason so many travelers recommend a given itinerary. Information you can only find locally, such as hidden eateries or Instagram-worthy hotspots, is like treasure you can discover in other travelers' itineraries.

You can also use the follow feature to follow users with similar travel tastes, so that every time they plan a new trip, you can see it in your feed. The itineraries of people who share your taste, whether travelers who love tracking down great food, prefer natural scenery, or are keen on history and culture, provide far more useful information than searching on your own.

After your trip, try sharing your own itinerary too. An itinerary revised after you have actually been there is a big help to other travelers. Sharing information such as places the AI recommended that were not great in reality, or good spots you happened upon that were not in the plan, raises the travel quality of the entire community. The beauty of travel is that the more you share, the more you gain.

Practical tip: Search for popular itineraries in myTravel's Discover tab, and use the ones you like as a reference to work into your own travel plan.

Wrap-up: Smart Travel with AI

AI travel planners are completely changing the way we plan trips. There is no longer any need to read through countless blogs and pore over a map to agonize over your route. But to use AI well, you need 5 core strategies: entering specific information, actively customizing, making use of weather and time zones, designing an efficient route, and using social features.

Use these 5 tips well, and AI becomes not just a simple itinerary-generation tool but your own personal travel consultant.
