1. What is myTravel?

myTravel is a smart travel planner app that uses AI to automatically create tailored trip itineraries for you.

2. Is it free to use?

myTravel is free to start. On the free plan you can generate AI itineraries up to 3 times per month, and all core features — editing, sharing, and weather — are available. For more AI generations or an ad-free experience, you can subscribe to Premium (from $3.99/month or $29.99/year).

3. Which devices can I use it on?

myTravel supports both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Download it free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, or on Google Play for Android devices. It is also available in the web browser.
